Ezra Creator Tools
A creator's own publishing toolkit

Publish your own videos to your own TikTok account — through the official API.

Ezra Creator Tools is a personal software tool built and operated by independent creator Guangyi Zhao. It connects the creator's own computer to his own TikTok account using TikTok's official Content Posting API, so finished videos can be uploaded and published without repetitive manual steps in the app. It works only for the account owner's own account and the owner's own original content.

What it does

Direct video publishing

Uploads finished video files from the creator's library and publishes them to his own TikTok profile via the official Content Posting API — no scraping, no unofficial clients.

Publishing status checks

Polls the API for the processing and publishing status of each upload, so the creator can confirm that a video went live and keep an accurate publishing log.

A steady daily workflow

Supports a daily publishing routine across the creator's series — short technology explainers, music videos of original hymns, and educational clips — with captions and AI-generated-content labels prepared in advance.

Owner-only by design

The tool is authorized once, by the account owner, through TikTok's standard OAuth flow. It stores only the owner's own access tokens, on the owner's own computer, and has no access to anyone else's account or data.

How it works

  1. Authorize once. The account owner signs in on TikTok's own authorization page and grants the tool permission to post to his account.
  2. Prepare content locally. Videos are written, recorded, and edited by the creator on his own computer — original scripts, original music, and AI-generated visuals and narration produced by the creator.
  3. Publish through the API. The tool uploads the finished file to the owner's account and publishes it with its caption, disclosing AI-generated content where the platform requires it.
  4. Verify and log. The tool checks the publishing status, and the result is recorded in the creator's publishing ledger. Access can be revoked at any time in TikTok's settings.

Who it is for

Ezra Creator Tools is a single-user tool. It is built for its operator, Guangyi Zhao — a San Jose, California based creator who publishes original technology, music, and educational videos under his own name. It is not a public service, is not offered to other users, and does not manage, post to, or read any third-party account.

  • Bible-story animation and other educational series written and produced by the creator.
