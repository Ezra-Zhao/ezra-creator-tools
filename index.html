Ezra Creator Tools
The publishing toolkit for TikTok creators

Ezra Creator Tools — publish your own videos to your own TikTok account, through the official API.

Ezra Creator Tools is a publishing tool for TikTok creators. A creator signs in with their own TikTok account and uses the tool to upload and publish their own original videos through TikTok's official Content Posting API — no scraping, no unofficial clients, no repetitive manual steps in the app. Every creator who uses the tool only ever accesses their own account and their own content.

What it does

Video publishing for creators

Creators upload finished video files from their own library and publish them to their own TikTok profile via the official Content Posting API, with captions and AI-generated-content labels prepared in advance.

Drafts and direct posting

Videos can be sent to the creator's own TikTok inbox as drafts for a final review tap, or published directly to the creator's profile once direct posting is enabled for the app.

Publishing status checks

The tool polls the API for the processing and publishing status of each upload, so creators can confirm that a video went live and keep an accurate publishing log.

Your account, your content

Each creator authorizes the tool with their own TikTok account through TikTok's standard OAuth flow. The tool stores only that creator's own access tokens, on the creator's own computer, and never accesses anyone else's account or data.

How it works

  1. Sign in with TikTok. The creator signs in on TikTok's own authorization page (Login Kit) and grants the tool permission to post to their account.
  2. Prepare content locally. Videos are written, recorded, and edited by the creator — original scripts, original music, and AI-generated visuals and narration produced by the creator.
  3. Publish through the API. The tool uploads the finished file to the creator's own account and publishes it with its caption, disclosing AI-generated content where the platform requires it.
  4. Verify and log. The tool checks the publishing status and records the result in the creator's publishing log. Access can be revoked at any time in TikTok's settings.

Who it is for

Ezra Creator Tools is for independent creators, musicians, educators, and small teams who produce original short-form video — technology explainers, original music, animation, and educational series — and want a reliable, official-API way to publish to their own TikTok accounts. Users publish only their own original content, in accordance with TikTok's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.
